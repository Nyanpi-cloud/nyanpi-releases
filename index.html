Everything around the episode

One app for what you watch, what you study and who you study with.

🎬

A player made for learners

Click a word for the dictionary, mine it to Anki with sentence, audio and screenshot. Japanese subtitles are fetched for you, and you can retime, restyle and resize them.

📚

Your AniList, your library

Syncs progress both ways, matches your local files, and finds missing episodes through qBittorrent.

🎯

Difficulty you can filter

Every show shows how hard its Japanese is. Filter the Den, Discover and collections to match your level.

Get started

Nyanpi is invite-only while it is small and friendly.

Get a code
Ask the person who told you about Nyanpi for an invite code.
Install
Windows: run Nyanpi-Setup.exe. Linux: extract the .tar.gz and start the Nyanpi program inside.
Enter the code
Pick a display name, then choose “I’m new” or “ready for immersion”.
Connect AniList
Optional, but it unlocks your profile, Den and Watch next.

Windows may warn that the installer is unsigned (“More info” → “Run anyway”). Updates install themselves on Windows; Linux builds are updated by downloading the new .tar.gz.

Honest expectations. Getting comfortable watching anime without subtitles (raw!) takes years of small daily steps. Nyanpi is a helper for that journey, not a shortcut: it makes each episode a little more useful, and it keeps the habit fun.
