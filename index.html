Everything around the episode

One app for what you watch, what you study and who you study with.

A player made for learners

Dictionary on every word, Anki mining with sentence, audio and screenshot, subtitles you can retime and restyle.

Your AniList, your library

Progress syncs both ways, local files are matched for you, and missing episodes can be fetched through qBittorrent.

Difficulty you can filter

Every show shows how hard its Japanese is. Narrow the Den, Discover and collections to your level.

Kana, grammar, kanji

Guided hiragana and katakana, grammar pages and a kanji browser. Already know the basics? Go straight to immersion.

A reader with a difficulty score

Read Japanese books with hover lookup and furigana. Aozora Bunko lists come ready-made.

Subtitles when there are none

No Japanese subtitles on Jimaku? Opt in to an offline speech model and make your own. Free, private, and honest about its mistakes.

Levels and achievements

Earn XP, unlock themes and name colours, and collect around a thousand achievements.

A small community

Chat, leaderboards, factions, and a look at who is watching what right now.

Get started

Nyanpi is invite-only while it is small and friendly.

Get a codeAsk the person who told you about Nyanpi for an invite code.
InstallWindows: run Nyanpi-Setup.exe. Linux: extract the .tar.gz and start the Nyanpi program inside.
Enter the codePick a display name, then choose “I’m new” or “ready for immersion”.
Connect AniListOptional, but it unlocks your profile, Den and Watch next.

Windows may warn that the installer is unsigned (“More info”, then “Run anyway”). Updates install themselves on Windows; on Linux, download the new .tar.gz.

Honest expectations. Getting comfortable watching anime without subtitles takes years of small daily steps. Nyanpi is a helper for that journey, not a shortcut: it makes each episode a little more useful and keeps the habit fun.
