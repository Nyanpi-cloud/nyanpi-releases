にゃんぴNyanpi

Watch anime and learn Japanese by understanding it. Nyanpi brings your AniList, a Japanese-subtitle player, kana and grammar lessons, and a small community together in one desktop app.

Download for Windows or Linux
Free, made for friends. Updates itself after install.

What it does

Your AniList, alive

Profile, stats, levels and a customisable dashboard, synced with your AniList account.

Built-in player

Plays your anime folders with Japanese subtitles (Jimaku). Click any word to look it up; one click sends it to Anki.

Learn Japanese by understanding

Guided hiragana and katakana in sets of five, grammar for recognition, a kanji browser, a reader and an Anki flow. No speaking or writing required.

Watch next

Suggestions based on what you already watch, with your Den of local shows and automatic missing-episode downloads through qBittorrent.

Friends, levels and clans

A chat that works like Discord, name colours that unlock as you level, leaderboards, and clans that compete each month.

Yours to arrange

Hide the tabs you do not use, pick a level-unlocked colour scheme, or switch to light mode. A tour shows you where everything is.

Realistic expectations. Following Japanese without subtitles is a long endurance race, measured in years. Nyanpi is built for the long run: small daily steps, recognising rather than producing, and plenty of anime in between.

Getting started

  1. 1
    Download

    Open the link above and download Nyanpi-Setup.exe. Windows may say it protected your PC because the app is not code-signed: choose More info, then Run anyway. Linux builds are shared as an AppImage.

  2. 2
    Connect AniList

    Open Settings and paste your AniList token, or just look around first. Everything is saved on your own computer.

  3. 3
    Take the tour

    The first launch offers a short guided tour. After that the app updates itself.

  4. 4
    Join the Social tab

    Optional. Ask me for an invite code to get chat, leaderboards and clans.
